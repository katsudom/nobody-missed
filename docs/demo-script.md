# Nobody Missed: demo script

**Pitch line:** "Everyone ranks requests. We chase the people who stop answering."

**One-liner:** State Farm checks on every policyholder in a storm by text and call. Anyone who goes quiet gets chased until a human is sent.

---

## Before you go on stage (~10 min)

1. **Phone:** text `JOIN <her name>` from the iPhone to the Photon number. Ringer and volume up, Do Not Disturb off.
2. **Projector laptop:** open the prod site in the admin browser.
3. Open **Dev**. Set the board to **Includes fake data**. Turn **Phone calls** on.
4. **End storm**, then **Reset**, so the board starts empty.
5. Optional: a teammate opens the site on a second laptop for the two-person claim.
6. Dry run once, then reset again.

## On stage (2:00)

| Time | Do | Say |
|---|---|---|
| **0:00** | Board is empty. | "When a storm hits, 911 is flooded with calls from people who are fine, and the people in real trouble often can't call at all." |
| **0:10** | Press **Start storm**. Hold up the phone when it dings. | "State Farm knows every policyholder in the storm zone, so we text all of them at once." |
| **0:15–0:40** | Point at rows turning **Safe**. Open a **Needs help** case, show its P1/P2/P3 and why. | "Most people reply 1 and drop off the list. Anyone who replies 2 is ranked by risk, and we show why." |
| **0:40** | Click **Take case**. Teammate clicks it on the same case. | "Only one responder gets it, so nobody shows up at the same house twice." |
| **0:45** | Point at her row, pinned at the top. The re-text arrives. | "The people who matter most are the ones who go quiet. She hasn't answered, so we text again." |
| **1:05** | **Phone rings.** Hold it up, don't answer. | "Still nothing, so we call." |
| **1:25** | Her row turns **red: Urgent, no contact**. | "No answer to a text, a re-text or a call. Now a person goes to check on her." |
| **1:30** | Take her case. | "Responders don't have to guess where to go first. The list tells them." |
| **1:40–2:00** | Close. | "Everyone ranks requests. We chase the people who stop answering." |

**If something goes wrong:**
- Phone doesn't ring: keep talking, point at the row's step status.
- Running long: skip the two-person claim at 0:40.
- Board looks empty: you're in "Real phones only". Switch to **Includes fake data**.

Timings assume the demo timers: re-text at 25 s, call 20 s later, urgent 20 s after that.
